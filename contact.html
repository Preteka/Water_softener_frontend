Frequently Asked Questions Before You Reach Out

Get quick clarity on our consultation process, water testing protocols, and emergency support policies.

Visit Knowledge Hub →

Is the in-home water test genuinely free?

Yes! Our diagnostic audit includes digital hardness testing, TDS measurement, chlorine/chloramine check, and iron testing at your kitchen and main inlet with zero obligation.

How quickly can you schedule an installation?

We maintain full inventory in all three regional hubs. Most residential systems can be professionally installed within 24 to 48 hours of initial consultation.

Do you service and repair other brand water softeners?

Yes. Our licensed technicians are trained on Fleck, Clack, Culligan, Kinetico, EcoWater, and generic control valves. We carry common rebuild kits and media resins.

What areas do your service trucks cover?

Our 3 regional hubs cover the entire Metro area, North Valley corridor, and Coastal County up to a 60-mile radius with no additional travel surcharges.
