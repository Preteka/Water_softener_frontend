HOW QUOTES WORK

What Influences a Water Treatment Quote?

Because every household water supply is unique, quotes are calculated using three verifiable engineering parameters.

1. Water Conditions

Your exact grains per gallon (GPG) hardness, total dissolved solids (TDS), and presence of iron, sulfur, or chloramines dictate the required resin type and filtration stages.

2. System Type & Capacity

Tank grain capacity (32,000 to 64,000+ grains) is sized to your household member count, daily water volume, and peak gallon-per-minute (GPM) flow rate.

3. Installation Requirements

Plumbing scope varies depending on whether your garage has an existing pre-plumbed softener loop or requires new copper/PEX line runs, drain routing, or electrical outlets.
