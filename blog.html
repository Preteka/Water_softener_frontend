WATER DIAGNOSTICS AT A GLANCE

Know What Your Water Is Telling You

Understanding three core dimensions our specialists analyze during an in-home water diagnostic.

1. Hardness (GPG)

Dissolved calcium and magnesium create abrasive limescale that clogs pipes, scales water heaters, and leaves cloudy deposits on bathroom tiles and fixtures.

2. Mineral Content & TDS

Total dissolved solids, municipal chlorine, chloramines, iron staining, and pH balance that alter water taste, cooking flavor, and cause pipe corrosion.

3. Filtration Needs

Matching whole-home ion-exchange conditioning for plumbing protection with multi-stage Reverse Osmosis for crisp, pure drinking water at the kitchen sink.
